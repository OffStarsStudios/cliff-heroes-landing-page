Plain-text version of the double opt-in confirmation email.

Brevo has a plain-text field alongside the HTML one. Filling it improves
deliverability (a text/plain part is one of the things spam filters look for)
and covers clients that refuse HTML. If left empty, Brevo auto-generates one
from the HTML, which usually reads badly.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Confirm your email

You asked to join the Cliff Heroes wishlist. Open the link below to
confirm it's really you, and you're on the list.

{{ params.DOIurl }}

After that we'll email you about closed-beta builds, season drops and
launch alerts - and nothing else.

Didn't sign up? Just ignore this email. You won't be added to anything
and we won't contact you again.

--
